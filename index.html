      How it works

      1. Pick a market, a direction and a stake. Tokenized stocks (TSLAx, NVDAx, AAPLx, MSTRx) or tokenized gold (XAUt), all open 24/7, long or short.
      2. The coin rolls on the live price. When the price goes your way it climbs a step, when it turns it steps down. Big moves, big jumps. Your leverage grows every 6 seconds, from 1x to 4x.
      3. Cash out whenever you want. If the price runs too far against you, the stairs give way and the coin falls. You lose your stake, never more.
      4. A real position. Long, your climb buys the token (real tokens in its escrow); short, it borrows and sells it. The game magnifies the moves (shown as "moves ×90"): the result splits what the tokens made from the game's boost.

      Sirocco Reserve

      Leverage is a real loan. At 3x your stake of 25 tUSDC is a 75 tUSDC position: 50 are borrowed from the Sirocco Reserve, a pool of tUSDC lent by anyone. The loan costs a 0.1% fee on the most you borrowed plus 36.5% APR for the seconds you held it, taken when you cash out. Win or lose, the reserve takes the other side, and you never owe more than your stake.

      Leverage packs

      Every 10 minutes, open a free pack. MagicBlock's verifiable randomness draws how high its ladder goes (Bronze 1.5x to Diamond 4x), and you play a 10 tUSDC climb on the house: you keep the gain.

      Devnet, test tokens only. Prices are read on a MagicBlock Ephemeral Rollup: every market trades around the clock at its token's live market price (the xStocks, and XAUt for gold). Real minute moves are small, so each market's move is boosted before leverage. Every climb is a Solana transaction you can check on the explorer.
